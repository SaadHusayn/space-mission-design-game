# 06 · Game Systems and Starter Data

> **Purpose:** a shared, balanced **starting point** so every builder doesn't have to invent numbers. You may tune values, but keep the **rules of thumb** (§1) so the trade-offs stay real.
> ⚠️ All numbers are **simplified, illustrative game values for learning**, not engineering data. Show "Simplified model" in-game.
> Covers: M3–M10, R3, R5, R8, R11, R14.

---

## 1. Rules of thumb (keep these true whatever you tune)

1. **Every part costs mass and money.** Most also cost power.
2. **More science = more mass, power, data and cost.** (This is the challenge's own example.)
3. **Farther destination → less launch capacity, weaker comms, (for Mars) less sunlight.**
4. **Eclipses need batteries.** Orbits with long shadows need bigger batteries.
5. **Data you can't downlink is lost science.** Comms must keep up with instruments.
6. **Bigger and better rockets carry more but cost more.**
7. **Lifetime is limited by the weakest part** (battery wear, radiation, orbit decay).
8. **At least 2 different designs must be able to win every scenario** (R5).

## 2. Resources (M7)

| Resource | Unit | Design-time meaning | Flight-time meaning | Limit / fail state |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Mass** | kg | Sum of all parts | n/a | ≤ bus structural limit **and** ≤ launch capacity to the orbit |
| **Power** | W | Avg load vs avg generation | Live: generation (sunlight, damage) vs load | Load > generation → battery drains → safe mode |
| **Budget** | $M | Parts + launch + operations (cost/yr × lifetime) | Event costs (delays, fixes) | Total ≤ objective budget |
| **Data** | Gb/day | Generated by instruments | Stored on board, then downlinked | Data > downlink → **lost science** |
| **Communications** | Gb/day | Downlink capacity at the destination | Ground-station passes | Too low → data lost. Zero → mission blind. |
| **Time** | years | Planned lifetime vs required lifetime | Mission day counter; parts wear out | Lifetime < required → objective partly failed |

Show **critical resources** per objective with a ★ (R14).

## 7. Formulas

```text
mass_total        = Σ part.mass
power_load        = Σ part.power                              (instruments count only when active)
power_gen         = solar.W × orbit.sunlight × (1 − orbit.eclipse) × health_factor
downlink_per_day  = comms.rate_Gb_per_h × orbit.contact_h × orbit.comms_factor
data_per_day      = Σ instrument.data
data_delivered    = min(data_per_day, downlink_per_day)   → lost = data_per_day − data_delivered
cost_total        = Σ part.cost + launch.cost + orbit.ops_cost × lifetime_planned
lifetime_planned  = min(battery.life(orbit), computer.life(orbit), orbit.max_life, objective.required_life_cap)
science_rate      = Σ instrument.science × fit(objective) × orbit.multiplier × pointing.multiplier
science_delivered = science_rate × (data_delivered / data_per_day) × uptime × min(1, lifetime_actual / lifetime_required)
```

### Feasibility checks (GO / NO-GO): M9

| Check | Rule | Message example |
|---|---|---|
| Launch mass | `mass_total ≤ launch.capacity[orbit]` | "✗ Too heavy: 1,135 kg > 800 kg for Light lifter to L2" |
| Structure | `mass_total ≤ bus.max_mass` | "✗ Small bus can't hold 950 kg. Choose a medium bus." |
| Budget | `cost_total ≤ objective.budget` | "⚠ Over budget: $452M > $450M" |
| Power | `power_load ≤ power_gen` | "✗ Instruments need 970 W, but you only make 744 W at Mars" |
| Battery | `battery.Wh ≥ power_load × eclipse_h` | "✗ Battery too small for the 0.6 h eclipse" |
| Pointing | ADCS works at the destination + meets the instrument's need | "✗ Magnetorquers don't work at L2" |
| Requirements | Required instrument types present | "✗ Lunar Science needs radar or an imager" |
| Comms | `downlink_per_day > 0` | "✗ No antenna: you'd never hear from your spacecraft" |

A NO-GO (✗) blocks launch. A WARN (⚠) allows launch with a penalty. **Always show the reason and a hint.**

### Flight simulation tick (M10)

Run one tick = **1 mission day** (fast-forward speeds: 1, 10, 100 days/sec).

```text
each day:
  gen  = power_gen (with damage from events, −2%/yr panel ageing)
  load = active loads
  battery += (gen − load) × 24 h   (clamp 0..capacity)
  if battery == 0 → SAFE MODE: instruments off, uptime loss
  stored  += data_per_day × uptime
  sent     = min(stored, downlink_per_day if ground pass available)
  stored  -= sent;  if stored > onboard_storage (e.g., 200 Gb) → overflow lost
  science += science_rate × (sent / data_per_day)
  roll random events (by difficulty)
  wear parts; end mission when lifetime reached or a critical part fails
```

## 8. Random events (R11)

| Event | Trigger chance | Effect | Player choices (trade-off) | Mitigated by |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| ☀️ **Solar storm** | Medium | Power −30% for 30 days | Safe mode (lose data) vs keep observing (risk the battery) | Large battery, rad-hard computer |
| 🌑 **Long eclipse season** | GEO / Lunar | Eclipse ×2 for 20 days | Reduce instruments vs drain the battery | Bigger battery |
| 💥 **Micrometeoroid hit** | Low | One solar panel −25% permanently | Re-point the panels (cost $) vs accept | Large array (margin) |
| ⚙️ **Reaction wheel failure** | Low (wheels) | Pointing degraded, science ×0.7 | Switch to backup mode (−power) vs accept | Precision ADCS (redundant) |
| 📡 **Ground station outage** | Medium | No downlink for 5 days | Store data (overflow risk) vs pause instruments | Big onboard storage, high-rate comms |
| 🧠 **Radiation upset** | Higher at GEO/L2/Mars | Computer reboot, 2 days of downtime | n/a | Rad-hard computer |
| 🛰️ **Debris warning** (LEO) | Low | Collision risk | Dodge (needs propulsion, uses fuel) vs gamble | Propulsion module |
| 🚀 **Launch delay** (weather) | Medium | Before launch | Wait (+$5M ops) vs launch in marginal weather (risk) | n/a |
| 🌟 **Bonus discovery** | High-science designs | Science +15% | Extend the mission (+cost) vs end as planned | High science value |

In team mode, events trigger a **team vote** ([07](07-team-mode-and-roles.md)).

## 9. Scoring (R3)

```text
Objectives achieved   35 %  = requirements met / total requirements
Science value         25 %  = min(1, science_delivered / objective.science_target)
Resource efficiency   20 %  = average of: budget margin (best 5–20 %), mass margin (best 5–25 %), power margin (best 10–30 %)
Collaboration / Decisions 20 % = team mode: consensus rate + every role used its decisions
                                 solo: quality of event decisions (did the choice protect the mission?)
Total = 0–100 → grade: S ≥95, A ≥85, B ≥70, C ≥55, F <55
Result: SUCCESS (all requirements met), PARTIAL (science ≥ 50 % of target), FAILURE (otherwise)
```

Margins that are **too large** score lower too (an oversized rocket = wasted money). This teaches that engineering is about balance.
